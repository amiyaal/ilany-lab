Amiyaal Ilany

Principal investigator amiyaal@tauex.tau.ac.il

Amiyaal studied biology and computer science at Tel Aviv University, where he also did his MSc and PhD in zoology. He was a postdoctoral fellow at the National Institute for Mathematical and Biological Synthesis at the University of Tennessee and then at the University of Pennsylvania. He ran his lab at Bar-Ilan University from 2016 and moved it to Tel Aviv University in 2024, where he is an associate professor.

He suspects he was born in the wrong era: 35 million years ago, Africa had hyraxes the size of a rhino.
