News

New papers, new people and other things worth telling.

  1. Oct 2026

    Nimrod Mazal Tov and Ayana Rosenthal joined the lab as MSc students. Nimrod is studying wolves and other predators around Ein Gedi, and Ayana is looking for the species hidden in DNA caught in spider webs.

  2. 2026

    With Mor Taub, Yoav Ram, Yossi Yovel and colleagues, we wrote in Current Biology about the challenge of decoding animal communication using AI.

  3. 2026

    Camille Bordes's field experiments are out in the Journal of Animal Ecology: how hyrax social networks respond to changes in predation risk and food.

  4. Feb 2026

    New tools for scoring rock hyrax behaviour in the field, with PhD student Prameek Kannan, published in PLOS ONE.

  5. Nov 2025

    We took part in a comparison of social networks across many wild animal systems, published in Nature Ecology & Evolution.

  6. Autumn 2025

    Shirel Lax joined the lab as an MSc student, to follow how the hyrax gut microbiome changes over time.

  7. 2024

    The lab moved to Tel Aviv University, to the School of Zoology and the Steinhardt Museum of Natural History.

  8. 2024

    Amiyaal wrote for young readers in Frontiers for Young Minds: what can the animals teach us about social relationships?

  9. Jul 2021

    Our study of social inheritance in spotted hyenas, with Kay Holekamp and Erol Akçay, was published in Science and made the journal's cover.
