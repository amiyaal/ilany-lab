Join us

We take students and postdocs at every level, for fieldwork, computational or theoretical projects on animal sociality.

Graduate students

MSc and PhD students can join through the School of Zoology, the School of the Environment or the Sagol School of Mind Sciences at Tel Aviv University. You can take part in fieldwork on the rock hyraxes of Ein Gedi, analyse the long-term data we already have, or build models of social behaviour. You are also welcome to propose a project on another study system.

Postdocs

We welcome postdocs who want to work on animal social networks, the social microbiome or communication, in the field or on theory. If you plan to apply for a fellowship, get in touch early so we can shape the proposal together.

How to apply

Write to Amiyaal with a few lines about what you would like to work on and why, and attach your CV.

amiyaal@tauex.tau.ac.il

Where we work

On campus we are in rooms 206 and 207 of the Sherman Building, Faculty of Life Sciences, Tel Aviv University.

In the field we work mostly at Ein Gedi Nature Reserve, on the western shore of the Dead Sea.
